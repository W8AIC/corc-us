General Ham Radio Resources

  • FCC – Find Part 97 rules on the Amateur service and renew your license
  • ARRL – Lots of information for the beginning or experienced ham
  • RFCEC – Information to teach yourself RF electronics principles
  • HamStudy.org – Practice ham radio tests
  • dstarusers.org – Good resource on D-STAR technology
  • dstarinfo.com – Good resource on D-STAR technology
  • M0UKD Slim Jim calculator – Learn how to build your own "Slim Jim" or J-Pole antenna; includes a form to calculate the dimensions of the antenna you want to build
  • Battery University – Lots of information about how batteries work
  • AC6V – A clearinghouse of links to amateur radio and commercial sites
  • Radio-Locator – Resource for looking up information about AM and FM broadcast stations; includes transmitter info and signal patterns
  • RC Groups – Resource for all things radio controlled
  • TechByter – (Mostly) computer-related site produced by local ham Bill Blinn, N8POV
  • The Ohio Simplex Net
  • All About Ham Radio for Beginners – More ham radio resources

Software Defined Ham Radio Links

  • Software-defined radio on Wikipedia – A site for general info
  • WebSDR – For more on interconnection to the internet by these radios
  • SDR-Radio.com – One other generic link on the subject
  • FlexRadio – The most popular and best selling transceiver systems
  • Nuand – Newer on the scene; kits of mostly board sets that can give you a lot of variety in the system you would build yourself
  • FUNcube Dongle – A very popular system for experimenters for several years now
  • NooElec Ham It Up up-converter – An up-converter that lets inexpensive DVB-T USB sticks listen to HF

Other suggestions

  • Check the equipment manufacturer's web page if looking for technical specs, manuals, etc.
  • When all else fails, use a search engine to search for information.
