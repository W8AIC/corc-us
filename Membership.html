Join Now

Membership entitles you to use of the "CORC" repeaters, regular copies of the CORC Newsletter, and a vote at the annual meeting of the corporation in January. CORC also has two other meetings, one in spring and the other one in fall. These are pot-luck type dinners, with different speakers and educational programs, and a good old rag-chew afterwards.

Membership dues
TermDues
1 year$18
2 years$32
3 years$45

Our dues have not increased in over 50 years.

Your membership also ensures that the directors have the funds necessary to repair and keep the repeaters on the air. This includes utility bills, normal operating expenses, and equipment upgrades and replacements. We also accept donations and are a 501(c)(3) charitable organization as established by the IRS (CORC's 501(c)(3) donation statement, PDF).

How to Join

Fill out the CORC membership application (PDF) and mail it with your payment to the address on the application, and you will become a member. You may also join in person at one of the events we have each year; the most recent meeting posted is listed on the home page.

Questions about membership? Contact the Membership Chairman, John Perone, W8RXX, through the Contact page.
