Join Now

Membership entitles you to use of the "CORC" repeaters, regular copies of the CORC Newsletter, and a vote at the annual meeting of the corporation in January. CORC also has  two other meetings, one in spring and the other one in fall. These are pot luck type dinners, with different speakers and educational programs, and a good old rag-chew afterwards.

Dues are $18 per year with multiple year discounts of $32 for two years and $45 for three. Our dues have not increased in over 50 years.

Your membership also insures that the directors have the funds necessary to repair and keep the repeaters on the air.  This includes utility bills and normal operating expenses and equipment upgrades and replacements.  We also accept donations and are now a 501(c)(3) charitable organization as established by the IRS.

To join, fill out the pdf version of the membership application.  Mail with your payment to the address on the application and you will become a member.

You may also join in person at one of the events we have each year. The next meeting is listed in the meeting notice.
