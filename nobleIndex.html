Repeaters

"CORC" owns and maintains amateur radio repeaters on the 2-meter, 6-meter and 70cm bands. The frequencies and callsigns of these repeaters, starting on 2-meter, are: 146.76, W8AIC/R; 146.97, W8RRJ/R; 147.33, W8NBA/R. On 6-meter, we have the W8RRJ/R repeater on 52.70 with 3 inputs on 52.94, 51.70 and 53.70, and on 70cm, we have the 444.200, W8AIC/R repeater. All of these repeaters use multiple receiver sites on commercial towers to ensure clear reception. All repeaters also encode 123.0 Hz tone PL on the output and decode on most receivers. The exception is the 444.200 repeater, which uses 151.4 Hz encode and decode. For best reception and to ensure accessing multiple receivers, please turn your PL tone on. These receiver sites are spread throughout Franklin, Licking, Delaware and Logan counties. Please refer to the Site Info page for more information.

Nets

The 146.76 repeater is host to the Central Ohio Severe Weather Net (COSWN), in which amateur radio "spotters" relay weather information directly to the National Weather Service in Wilmington, Ohio, through a controlled net. This information is used to help the NWS in deciding where and when to issue severe watches and warnings. The Weather Watch holds its weekly training nets on Tuesdays at 7:30 PM during the spring and summer months on the .76 machine. The net is also brought up any time it is requested by the National Weather Service.

The 146.97 repeater is the primary frequency for the Central Ohio Traffic Net (COTN), which meets daily at 7:15 PM. The first alternate frequency is 146.76 and the second alternate frequency is 147.33.

The 147.33 repeater hosts IRLP Node 8094, which links it to other nodes all over the world. Please refer to the IRLP page for more linking information. (Note: corc.us currently lists the IRLP node as temporarily off the air.)

The CORC Tech Net meets every other Sunday at 7:30 PM on the 146.760 repeater (123.0 Hz PL) for discussion of ham radio topics on a technical level. All amateurs are welcome. See the Tech Net page for the schedule and past presentations.

Central Ohio Radio Club sponsored ARRL VE Test Schedule for 2027

The 2027 schedule will also be posted on the www.ae8fp.net website, where the most up-to-date information is published. All tests are held on Saturdays:

  • January 16, 2027
  • April 17, 2027
  • June 26, 2027 (Field Day)
  • July 17, 2027
  • October 16, 2027
  • December 11, 2027 ('Twas a HAM before Christmas)

All sessions will be pre-registration only. It is now an FCC requirement to have an FCC FRN, an active email address and an active phone number before taking any exam. Please bring your FCC FRN, original license, a copy of your license (if a licensed ham), a valid photo ID and $15.00 (cash preferred).

For all exam sessions: effective April 19, 2022, the FCC charges a $35.00 application fee for amateur radio licenses. The fee applies to new, renewal, rule waiver, and modification applications that request a new vanity call sign. License upgrades do not have a fee applied. The fee is per application. VEs will not collect the fees at exam sessions.

Time and location for most of these dates are 10:00 AM at Westerville Fire Station #111, 400 W. Main St., Westerville, Ohio. If you are homebound and in need of a test team to make a visit, please contact Frank Piper, AE8FP, through www.ae8fp.net or the Contact page to make arrangements.
